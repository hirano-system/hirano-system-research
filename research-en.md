From Man-Month, Labor-Intensive, Multi-Tier Subcontracting to Success-Fee, Knowledge-Intensive System Development
Research Overview

This research has been conducted since 1986 with the aim of transforming conventional man-month-based, labor-intensive, multi-tier subcontracting system development into success-fee-based, knowledge-intensive system development.

In conventional system development, the scale of development has generally been estimated in man-months, with personnel assigned according to the estimated workload. However, as systems have become more complex and IT has become increasingly connected with business management, it has become important not only to complete a system, but also to clarify what results and business benefits are generated by IT investment.

This research therefore examines IT investment planning, system development, effect measurement, evaluation, contracts, organizational design, and risk management as an integrated framework. Its objective is to establish a success-fee-based system development model in which the value of system development is evaluated in relation to the customer's results and business benefits.

The research also integrates software engineering, management and accounting, contract theory, organizational theory, and AI-based evaluation. The goal is to transform system development from a business based primarily on the deployment of personnel and man-months into a knowledge-intensive business that creates value together with customers through the accumulation, sharing, and reuse of knowledge.

Research Period

The research began with practical experience in system development in 1986 and has continued through research and practical activities from 1986 to the present.

Major research themes have included:

Software engineering and system development processes
IT investment assessment and effect measurement
Success-fee-based contract theory
Evaluation theory and AI-based assessment
Management and accounting
Organizational theory
Risk analysis and risk management
Knowledge-intensive system development
