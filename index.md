About This Research

This website presents the research of Naohiko Hirano on system development, software engineering, IT investment assessment, success-based system development, and knowledge-intensive organizations.

The research has evolved continuously since 1986.

Its central theme is the transformation of system development from a man-month-based and labor-intensive business model toward an outcome-based, success-fee-based, and knowledge-intensive model.

From Man-Month-Based Development to Outcome-Based Development

Traditional system development has often been based on the amount of labor required to develop software.

This research explores a different approach:

System development should be evaluated and rewarded according to the outcomes and value created for the customer.

This requires the integration of system development with:

IT investment assessment

Business outcomes

Value creation

Risk management

Contracts and incentives

Organizational design

Knowledge creation

Artificial intelligence

Research History
1986–1994 — Systems Engineering and Software Engineering

Research into systems engineering, software engineering, and improving the way information systems are developed.

1995–2001 — IT Investment Assessment

Research expanded from software development to the question of how IT investment creates economic and business value.

2001 — Success-Fee-Based Software Development

Research into a business model in which software development compensation is connected to customer success rather than simply to development effort or man-months.

2001–2018 — Contracts, Risk, Organizations, and Value Creation

Research expanded to contracts, risk sharing, organizational structures, knowledge creation, and the relationship between system development and business results.

2018 — Success-Based System Development

The concept of success-based system development was further developed and organized into a comprehensive business model.

2022 — Success-Based System Development and IT Investment Assessment

Success-based system development was integrated with IT investment assessment in order to connect system development with measurable business outcomes.

Present — Generative AI and Knowledge-Intensive System Development

Generative AI and AI agents are creating new possibilities for implementing concepts that have been studied for decades.

Current Research

The current research focuses on the integration of:

AI × IT Investment Assessment × Success Fee × Knowledge-Intensive Organization

Generative AI and AI agents may make it possible to implement knowledge-intensive system development, automated IT investment assessment, digital twins, and outcome-based business models.

The objective is not simply to develop software faster.

The broader objective is to explore a new model of system development in which technology, business outcomes, investment, risk, contracts, organizations, and knowledge are integrated.
