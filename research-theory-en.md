Research Theory and Framework
1. System Development Process Theory

A central theme of this research is the development of a systematic theory of the entire system development process.

Conventional system development tends to divide work into separate stages and assign personnel according to estimated workload. This research instead examines planning, design, development, testing, implementation, and operation as an integrated process.

The objective is to establish a process in which the expected business results, risks, responsibilities, and evaluation methods are considered from the beginning of system planning.

2. IT Investment Assessment and Effect Measurement

When IT investment is considered from the customer's perspective, it is not sufficient to explain what system will be constructed.

It is necessary to clarify:

Why the investment is necessary
What business results are expected
How the results will be measured
How the investment will be recovered
How risks will be managed
How actual results will be compared with the original plan

This research therefore treats IT investment assessment and effect measurement as fundamental elements of system development.

3. Success-Fee-Based Contract Theory

A transition from man-month-based development to success-fee-based development requires a fundamental change in the contract.

In a man-month model, compensation is primarily associated with the amount of labor and development time.

In a success-fee model, compensation is connected with the results and value created for the customer.

This requires agreements concerning:

Expected results
Measurement and evaluation methods
Distribution of benefits
Responsibilities and authority
Risks
Conditions for settlement
Information sharing
Changes in the business environment

Contract theory and game theory are therefore important components of the research.

The contract must provide a framework in which both the customer and the system development organization can understand how value, responsibility, and risk are shared.

4. Effect Measurement and Evaluation Theory

One of the most difficult issues in success-fee-based development is determining whether the system actually produced the expected results.

The research therefore examines methods for measuring and evaluating the effects of IT investment.

Evaluation must connect system activities with business results rather than simply measuring the amount of software produced.

The research also explores the use of artificial intelligence and AI agents for evaluation, together with a digital-twin approach to representing management and business activities.

5. Connecting Management, Accounting, and IT

IT systems are not independent from business management.

They affect business processes, resources, costs, revenues, productivity, decision-making, and organizational activities.

This research therefore integrates perspectives from:

Management
Industrial engineering
Accounting
Finance
Information technology
Data analysis
Artificial intelligence

The objective is to understand IT investment and its effects as part of the overall management system.

The research also considers the relationship between IT and non-IT resources, activities, costs, and contributions to profit.

6. Organizational Theory

Success-fee-based system development requires an organization centered on customer outcomes rather than simply the assignment of personnel.

Because results are connected with the customer's management and profitability, risk analysis and risk management are also essential.

Risks must be identified and managed across the entire process:

Planning
Design
Development
Testing
Implementation
Operation

The organization therefore needs:

An outcome-centered structure
Clear authority and responsibility
Cross-functional collaboration
Knowledge sharing and accumulation
Human-resource and organizational development focused on outcomes
Risk analysis and risk management
Continuous management of plans, actual results, and evaluations

Results, responsibility, authority, and risk should be managed as an integrated system.

7. Knowledge-Intensive System Development

The fundamental objective of the research is to transform system development from a labor-intensive business into a knowledge-intensive business.

In a man-month-based model, sales are closely related to the number of people assigned and the amount of development time.

In a knowledge-intensive model, value is created through:

Accumulated knowledge
Shared knowledge
Reusable knowledge
Methods and methodologies
Tools
Evaluation techniques
Experience
Collaboration with customers

The objective is not simply to build more software with fewer people.

It is to create greater customer value through the systematic accumulation, sharing, reuse, and application of knowledge.

8. Overall Structure of Success-Fee-Based System Development

The research proposes an integrated structure consisting of the following elements:

Customer Value

Clarification of the business objectives and expected results of IT investment.

Planning and Assessment

Planning of investment, expected effects, measurement methods, and recovery of investment.

System Development

Integrated management of planning, design, development, testing, implementation, and operation.

Effect Measurement and Evaluation

Measurement of actual results and comparison with the original plan.

Contract

Definition of responsibilities, authority, risks, measurement methods, and distribution of benefits.

Organization

Teams, authority, knowledge sharing, risk analysis, and risk management designed to create results.

Knowledge

Accumulation, sharing, reuse, and continuous creation of knowledge.

AI and Digital Twins

Use of AI-based evaluation and digital representations of management and business activities to support decision-making and effect measurement.

These elements are not independent. They form an integrated framework for creating and evaluating customer value.

9. Research Achievement

By 2018, the research had reached a framework covering process theory, contract theory, construction technology, AI-agent-based evaluation using management digital twins, and organizational theory for realizing success-fee-based system development.

The research therefore goes beyond a proposal to change the billing method from man-months to success fees.

It proposes a broader transformation of the system development business itself:

from labor-based development → to knowledge-intensive development → to outcome-oriented development centered on customer value.

10. Future Research

The research continues in the environment created by generative AI and increasingly AI-assisted software development.

Future research focuses on how AI can be integrated with:

System development processes
IT investment assessment
Effect measurement
Contract management
Risk management
Organizational management
Knowledge accumulation and reuse

The long-term objective is to establish an integrated model in which people, AI, technology, organizations, contracts, and management work together to create measurable value for customers.
